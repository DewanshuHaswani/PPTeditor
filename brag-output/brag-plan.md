# Brag Plan: All Hands

## Planning rubric
1. App: a presentation editor with animated live views, local saves and portable offline sharing.
2. Strongest capability: an entire live deck travels in a ZIP and opens offline after extraction.
3. Visual hook: the actual glass presentation panels cascading from editor into live viewing.
4. Actual UI: editor typing/live preview, objects and image settings, live navigation, Story/Movie, timer, save/export.
5. Shortest satisfying feature film: 92 seconds. User asks comprehensive explanation; this explicitly overrides the skill’s default 15–25 second teaser.
6. Tone: polished with cinematic movement and warm clarity.
7. Audio: English Kokoro af_heart narration, warm rhythmic bed, motion-matched clicks, soft card slides, keyboard details and low reveal impacts.
8. Share caption: Make the room lean in. All Hands brings editable slides, live presentation modes, local saves and an offline web deck into one place.
9. Flow: edit a fictional workshop deck → present it in live modes → save locally and share an offline ZIP.

## Angle
Ideas in motion: show an idea becoming an editable deck, a confident live session, and an experience anyone can open. Every capability is grounded in README and implementation. No AI slide generation, PPTX export, automatic emailing, open-source or creator claims. Fictional demonstration deck avoids real organization and person data.

## Reference
The supplied 97-second Napkin film uses a light canvas, large plain-language chapters, floating slide galleries, and close UI demonstrations. Borrow the readable choreography and feature-led pacing, not its branding, audio, footage, or unsupported capabilities.

## Visual identity
Light warm canvas #f3f3ef, dark text #15192b, product indigo #4f46e5, accent #b7c2ff. Actual product glass slides retain their native colors. Ship local fonts and GSAP. Display: editorial serif mixed with clear bold sans. Screenshots retain original UI fonts.

## Format
1920×1080, 16:9, 30 fps, 92 seconds.

## Audio direction
Brag narration route: local Kokoro af_heart (user asks feature explanations). Bundled vol-12 music, steady clean 109.96 BPM. Music at low narration support; swell in breathing gaps, fade out over final hold. Warm soft impact for the reveal, card-slide and switch accents for moving UI, sparse keypress/click details. Audio-reactive subtle accent presence from extracted music RMS, never a spectrum display.

## Music cue guidance
Read bundled vol-12 preset. First editor reveal can land at 6.00s, feature proof at 8.74s, first object row at 14.20s with 15.29/16.38 following. Later timings use natural narrative and readable holds; do not force text onto every beat.
